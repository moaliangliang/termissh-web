Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

1. Introduction

TermiSSH (the "App") is an SSH terminal and SFTP file manager developed by Maoliang Liang (the "Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the App to minimize data collection.

TL;DR: TermiSSH does NOT collect any personal data, analytics, or usage statistics. Your credentials stay on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information — We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.
  • No analytics — We do not use any analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or crash reporting tools.
  • No advertising — The App contains no advertisements or ad-related SDKs.
  • No third-party data sharing — We do not share any data with third parties.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your device's Keychain and local file system, and never transmitted to us or any third party:

  • SSH server credentials (passwords and private keys) — stored in iOS Keychain with hardware encryption.
  • Server metadata (hostname, port, username, labels, group names) — stored in the app's sandboxed Documents directory as JSON.
  • Terminal themes and preferences — stored in UserDefaults.
  • App lock passcode — stored in iOS Keychain.

4. iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync (which is optional and Pro-only), server metadata (hostname, port, username, labels) will be synced across your devices via Apple's CloudKit. This data is end-to-end encrypted by Apple. Passwords and private keys are never synced to iCloud.

5. Network Traffic

All SSH and SFTP traffic is encrypted end-to-end using the libssh2 library with mbedTLS cryptography. The Developer does not have access to your network traffic. The App does not make any network connections other than the SSH/SFTP connections you explicitly initiate.

6. Face ID / Touch ID

The App offers optional Face ID or Touch ID protection for app launch. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and is not accessible to the App or the Developer.

7. Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple's App Store In-App Purchase system. The Developer does not receive any payment information. Transaction identifiers are stored locally for receipt validation purposes only.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete all data stored by the App by uninstalling the App. Server credentials stored in Keychain will be removed automatically upon uninstallation. You may also delete individual servers or clear all data from within the App's settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

maoliangliang.work@outlook.com

12. Export Compliance

TermiSSH uses encryption (libssh2 + mbedTLS) exclusively for SSH protocol connections. This is classified as "mass market" encryption under BIS Category 5 Part 2. An annual self-classification report is filed with the U.S. government as required.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 6 月 1 日

1. 引言

TermiSSH（以下简称"本 App"）是一款 SSH 终端和 SFTP 文件管理工具，由毛亮亮（以下简称"开发者"）开发。本隐私政策说明了本 App 如何处理您的数据。我们非常重视您的隐私，并在设计上最大程度减少了数据收集。

一句话总结：TermiSSH 不收集任何个人数据、分析数据或使用统计数据。您的凭据仅保存在您的设备上。

2. 我们不收集的数据

  • 无个人信息 — 我们不收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话号码或任何个人身份信息。
  • 无分析统计 — 我们不使用任何分析 SDK、追踪代码或崩溃报告工具。
  • 无广告 — 本 App 不包含任何广告或广告相关 SDK。
  • 无第三方数据共享 — 我们不会与任何第三方共享数据。

3. 存储在您设备上的数据

以下数据仅存储在您设备的 Keychain 和本地文件系统中，绝不会传输给我们或任何第三方：

  • SSH 服务器凭据（密码和私钥）— 存储在 iOS Keychain 中，硬件加密保护。
  • 服务器元数据（主机名、端口、用户名、标签、分组名称）— 存储在 App 沙盒 Documents 目录的 JSON 文件中。
  • 终端主题和偏好设置— 存储在 UserDefaults 中。
  • 应用锁密码— 存储在 iOS Keychain 中。

4. iCloud 同步

如果您启用 iCloud 同步（可选功能，需要 Pro 订阅），服务器元数据（主机名、端口、用户名、标签）将通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 在您的设备间同步。Apple 对此数据进行端到端加密。密码和私钥不会同步到 iCloud。

5. 网络通信

所有 SSH 和 SFTP 通信均使用 libssh2 库和 mbedTLS 加密协议进行端到端加密。开发者无法访问您的网络流量。本 App 不会发起除您主动建立的 SSH/SFTP 连接之外的任何网络连接。

6. Face ID / Touch ID

本 App 提供可选的 Face ID 或 Touch ID 保护。生物识别数据完全由 iOS 处理，App 和开发者均无法访问。

7. 订阅

Pro 订阅通过 Apple 的 App Store 内购系统处理。开发者不会收到任何支付信息。交易标识符仅本地存储用于收据验证。

8. 数据删除

卸载本 App 即可删除所有存储的数据。Keychain 中存储的服务器凭据将在卸载时自动移除。您也可以在 App 设置中删除单个服务器或清除所有数据。

9. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会故意收集任何儿童的个人信息。

10. 本政策的变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。变更将发布在此页面，并更新"最后更新"日期。

11. 联系方式

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问或关切，请通过以下方式联系我们：

maoliangliang.work@outlook.com

12. 出口合规

TermiSSH 使用加密技术（libssh2 + mbedTLS）仅用于 SSH 协议连接，属于 BIS 第 5 类第 2 部分的"批量市场"加密产品。每年按要求向美国政府提交自我分类报告。
